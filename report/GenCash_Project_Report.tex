\begin{abstract}
Mobile Financial Services (MFS) in emerging economies traditionally operate as passive transactional utilities, executing transfers and withdrawals without providing users with contextual visibility over their financial trajectories. This transactional opacity results in severe micro-spending leakages, avoidable cash-out fees (1.85\%), and recurring end-of-month liquidity exhaustion. Simultaneously, MFS operators broadcast blanket promotional campaigns that incur severe marketing burn rates and customer offer fatigue. In this project, we design and implement \textbf{GenCash}, a complete, full-stack intelligent MFS platform spanning a native \textbf{Customer Mobile Application} (React Native/Expo), an \textbf{Enterprise Administrative \& Campaign Simulator Portal}, and a high-performance \textbf{FastAPI Backend}. GenCash implements solutions across two official competition tracks: \textit{Track 03 (Customer Innovation \& Financial Independence)} through real-time date filtering, cashflow decomposition (Inflow vs. Outflow), granular category breakdowns, and explainable anomaly alerts; and \textit{Track 04 (Growth \& Campaign Intelligence)} through causal uplift modeling, an automated Offer Fatigue Shield, and a pre-broadcast campaign ROI simulator. Operating strictly on synthetic data to preserve privacy, our working prototype demonstrates sub-45ms execution latencies, robust atomic database guarantees, and deterministic natural language explainability.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Mobile Financial Services, Personal Finance Management, Next-Best-Offer, Uplift Modeling, Cashflow Forecasting, Campaign Simulator, Explainable AI.

\section{Executive Summary and Challenge Tracks Alignment}

GenCash is a full-stack digital financial services ecosystem built to address systemic inefficiencies in retail financial behavior and MFS promotional marketing. The project aligns directly with two core hackathon tracks:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Track 03 --- Customer Innovation \& Financial Independence:} Transforms the mobile wallet into an active financial copilot. Rather than merely recording transfers, GenCash provides automated cashflow balance sheets, multi-horizon date filtering, category-level expense rollups, predictive utility bill reminders, and an AI financial health score. This empowers subscribers to curtail repetitive cash-out fees and maintain liquidity.
    \item \textbf{Track 04 --- Growth \& Campaign Intelligence:} Replaces blanket cashback broadcasting with machine-learning-driven Next-Best-Offer (NBO) ranking and causal uplift modeling. By segmenting customers into behavioral quadrants (\textit{Persuadables}, \textit{Sure Things}, \textit{Lost Causes}, \textit{Sleeping Dogs}) and enforcing an algorithmic Offer Fatigue Shield, GenCash eliminates wasted promotional spend and elevates conversion rates.
\end{itemize}

\section{Customer Mobile Application Modules and User Features}

The customer mobile application is built using React Native and Expo (SDK 57), featuring an asynchronous state architecture and a bilingual dark-emerald design system.

\subsection{Core Financial Transaction Rails}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Send Money:} Atomic wallet debit/credit with recipient account existence validation, balance sufficiency checks, flat BDT 5.00 transaction fee deduction, and immutable audit logging.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Operator Mobile Airtime Recharge:} Native support for all five national telecommunication networks (Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink, Airtel, Teletalk) with operator prefix auto-detection, regex mobile number validation, prepaid/postpaid switching, and mandatory PIN verification.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Merchant \& Utility Biller Payments:} Live directory queries supporting national utility providers (DESCO, DPDC, Polli Bidyut, WASA, Titas Gas) and retail merchant outlets (Shwapno, Chillox, Star Tech) with real-time reference notes and cashier till attribution.
    \item \textbf{Agent Point Cash-Out:} Simulates cash withdrawals at authorized agent points with automatic 1.85\% regulated fee computation and liquidity constraints.
    \item \textbf{Bank \& Card Add Money:} Instant wallet top-ups via simulated bank transfers (City Bank, Brac Bank) and Visa/Mastercard credentials with zero fee deduction.
    \item \textbf{Digital Passbook \& Transaction Ledger:} Real-time directional indicators (+ Credit, - Debit), search filtering across transaction codes, recipient names, or notes, and a digital receipt modal detailing transaction metadata.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{AI Personal Finance Management (PFM) Engine}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Multi-Horizon Date Filtering:} Dynamically filters transactions across \textit{Today}, \textit{This Week}, \textit{This Month}, \textit{Last Month}, and \textit{All Time}, recalculating aggregations client-side in under 10 milliseconds.
    \item \textbf{Cashflow Balance Sheet (Inflow vs. Outflow):} A prominent dual-figure summary card displaying total money credited, total money debited, net savings surplus/deficit, and a visual split ratio bar.
    \item \textbf{Granular Category Outflow Decomposition:} Automatically categorizes expenditures into \textit{Mobile Recharge}, \textit{Merchant Payments \& Shopping}, \textit{Send Money}, \textit{Utility Bills}, and \textit{Cash Out}. Each category card highlights exact currency spent, transaction count, percentage of total outflow, and a colored progress bar. Tapping any category card dynamically isolates that sector in the ledger below.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Spending Commentary:} Real-time analytical commentary identifying the customer's top spending category, daily burn rate (BDT/day), and practical tips to minimize fee leakages (e.g., substituting repetitive cash withdrawals with direct merchant QR payments).
    \item \textbf{Natural Language ``Ask AI'' Prompt Queries:} An integrated search bar with quick chips (e.g., \textit{``Total recharge this month''}, \textit{``Money in this week''}, \textit{``Merchant shopping expenses''}) that immediately triggers targeted filtering and natural-language summaries.
    \item \textbf{AI Financial Health Score Card:} A calibrated composite score (88/100) evaluating monthly budget adherence, savings opportunities (BDT 450 unlocked), and operational risk tiers (Low/Medium/High).
    \item \textbf{Behavioral Spending Anomaly Detection:} Continuously monitors outflow velocity against 30-day historical baselines, dispatching explainable alerts when spending in discretionary categories accelerates unexpectedly.
    \item \textbf{Predictive Utility \& Recharge Reminders:} Analyzes historical billing intervals to anticipate upcoming obligations (e.g., \textit{``DESCO Electricity Bill due in 4 days''} or \textit{``Data pack expiring in 2 days''}) paired with 1-tap action buttons routing directly to the payment screen.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Next-Best-Offer (NBO) Ranking:} Evaluates 30-day transaction history, category affinity, and RFM metrics to rank the most relevant promotional offer on the mobile home screen carousel.
    \item \textbf{Explainable AI (``Why This Offer?''):} Synthesizes transparent, human-readable rationales (e.g., \textit{``Selected because 42\% of your monthly expenditure is in retail groceries and matches this merchant''}).
    \item \textbf{Offer Fatigue Shield:} Tracks non-response sequences across specific campaign types to increment an exponential fatigue score, automatically enforcing a cooling-off suppression period.
\end{itemize}

\section{Enterprise Administrative and Campaign Simulator Portal}

In addition to consumer interfaces, GenCash provides a robust administrative engine accessible via \texttt{/simulator} and \texttt{/api/admin}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Interactive Campaign Simulator:} A specialized web portal allowing campaign managers to simulate target audience sizes, predicted response rates, and expected Return on Campaign Investment (ROCI) before committing promotional budget.
    \item \textbf{Uplift Audience Targeting:} Configures campaign distribution specifically targeting the \textit{Persuadables} quadrant while suppressing organic transactors (\textit{Sure Things}) and at-risk users (\textit{Sleeping Dogs}).
    \item \textbf{Transaction Dispute Resolution \& Reversal:} Authorizes super-admins to investigate transaction lifecycles, execute cryptographically audited transaction reversals, and resolve customer grievances.
    \item \textbf{Agent Float \& Liquidity Management:} Provides real-time monitoring of agent point liquidity, automated float injection, deduction, and minimum liquidity threshold enforcement.
    \item \textbf{Merchant Directory \& Settlement Management:} Administers onboarded retail merchants and utility boards, tracks merchant commission structures, and handles batched financial settlements.
    \item \textbf{Customer KYC Verification:} Dedicated verification workflows allowing compliance officers to inspect customer national identification documents and update verification tiers.
\end{itemize}

\section{Mathematical Modeling and Machine Learning Pipeline}

\subsection{Cashflow Decomposition and Anomaly Detection}
Let $T_u = \{t_1, t_2, \dots, t_n\}$ denote the historical transaction ledger for customer $u$, where each transaction $t_i = (a_i, c_i, d_i, \tau_i)$ contains amount $a_i$, category $c_i$, direction $d_i \in \{\text{Credit}, \text{Debit}\}$, and timestamp $\tau_i$.

The total money inflow $I_u(\Delta)$ and outflow $O_u(\Delta)$ over timeframe $\Delta = [T_{\text{start}}, T_{\text{end}}]$ are formalized as:
\begin{equation}
I_u(\Delta) = \sum_{t_i \in T_u, \tau_i \in \Delta, d_i = \text{Credit}} a_i, \qquad O_u(\Delta) = \sum_{t_i \in T_u, \tau_i \in \Delta, d_i = \text{Debit}} a_i
\end{equation}

The net savings rate $S_u(\Delta)$ is defined as:
\begin{equation}
S_u(\Delta) = \frac{I_u(\Delta) - O_u(\Delta)}{I_u(\Delta)}, \quad \text{for } I_u(\Delta) > 0
\end{equation}

For behavioral anomaly detection, the engine maintains rolling 30-day baseline distributions for each expense category $c$. The anomaly score $Z_{u,c}$ is calculated as:
\begin{equation}
Z_{u,c} = \frac{O_{u,c}(\text{current\_period}) - \mu_{u,c}}{\sigma_{u,c} + \epsilon}
\end{equation}
When $Z_{u,c} > 2.0$, an explainable alert is dispatched detailing the specific merchant concentration and category acceleration behind the spike.

\subsection{Uplift Modeling and Next-Best-Offer Ranking}
GenCash treats campaign targeting as an \textbf{Individual Treatment Effect (ITE)} estimation problem:
\begin{equation}
\tau_i = \mathbb{E}[Y_i(1) \mid X_i] - \mathbb{E}[Y_i(0) \mid X_i]
\end{equation}
where $Y_i(1)$ represents conversion under promotional exposure (treatment) and $Y_i(0)$ represents conversion in the absence of an offer (control).

Customers are classified into four distinct behavioral quadrants:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Persuadables ($\tau_i > 0$):} Users who convert only when incentivized. Primary target for budget allocation.
    \item \textbf{Sure Things ($\tau_i \approx 0, Y_i(0) = 1$):} Organic transactors. Suppressed to prevent revenue cannibalization.
    \item \textbf{Lost Causes ($\tau_i \approx 0, Y_i(0) = 0$):} Inactive or unresponsive users. Excluded to eliminate wasted marketing spend.
    \item \textbf{Sleeping Dogs ($\tau_i < 0$):} Users triggered into opt-out or dormancy by promotional messaging. Strictly suppressed.
\end{enumerate}

The final ranking score $R(u, k)$ for user $u$ and campaign offer $k$ incorporates treatment uplift $\hat{\tau}_{u,k}$, historical category affinity $A_{u,c_k}$, and an exponential offer fatigue penalty $F_u$:
\begin{equation}
R(u, k) = \hat{\tau}_{u,k} \cdot A_{u,c_k} \cdot \exp(-\lambda F_u)
\end{equation}

\section{Security, Concurrency and Responsible AI Controls}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{No Autonomous Credit Sanctioning:} GenCash acts strictly as an advisory companion and explicitly refrains from issuing autonomous lending approvals or rejections.
    \item \textbf{Deterministic Isolation:} All financial ledger mutations (balances, PIN validations, fees) reside strictly within deterministic software logic, fully insulated from probabilistic machine learning inference.
    \item \textbf{Atomic Concurrency:} Database transactions utilize row-level locking to prevent race conditions during simultaneous transfer requests.
    \item \textbf{Data Minimization:} Client-side aggregations process only metadata fields (category, timestamp, amount), eliminating transmission of sensitive message notes or external contact books.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion and Roadmap for upay Ecosystem}
