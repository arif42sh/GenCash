MARKET DEFICIENCIES Industry Problem Formulation
02 / 14
Problem Formulation

The Dual Inefficiency in Emerging Market MFS

Current MFS platforms operate as dumb transactional pipes, causing consumer cash leakage and operator marketing waste.

Consumer Pain Points (Track 03)
Financial Opacity & Avoidable Fee Burn
  • Passive Transaction Rails: Users send, cash-out, and recharge without real-time contextual tracking of their net liquidity.
  • Avoidable Fee Burn: Up to 1.85% is lost on routine cash-outs because users lack balance visibility and direct merchant QR payment prompts.
  • Month-End Liquidity Shocks: Unplanned micro-expenses cause sudden wallet depletion and late utility bill defaults (DESCO/DPDC).
  • Retrospective SMS Alerts: SMS notifications only state what already happened, offering zero proactive budgeting guidance or fee optimization.
Operator Bottlenecks (Track 04)
Scattergun Promotions & Subsidized Organic Spend
  • Blanket Broadcasting: Generic cashback promotions blasted across entire user bases regardless of user transaction affinity.
  • Subsidizing Organic Conversions: Rewarding "Sure Things" who would have transacted anyway destroys marketing ROI and operator margins.
  • Promotional Fatigue & Churn: Continuous irrelevant SMS and push spam trigger notification muting and app uninstalls.
  • Zero Pre-Flight Validation: Campaign managers commit millions in marketing budget with no ability to simulate conversion lift before launch.
Slide 2 Script (The Problem): Ask the judges: Why do users need AI in an MFS app? Every month, millions of Bangladeshi users ask 'Where did my salary go?' They lose up to 1.85% on cashouts simply because no app nudges them to pay merchants directly via QR. On the flip side, operators like upay burn millions sending generic 20 Tk cashback SMS to users who were going to recharge anyway. This dual inefficiency hurts both customer retention and operator EBITDA.

TRACK 04 INTELLIGENCE Growth & Campaign Optimization

Precision Targeting Engine

Causal Uplift Modeling & Four-Quadrant Segmentation

Eliminating marketing waste by modeling Individual Treatment Effect (ITE) rather than basic response correlation.

Theoretical Formulation
Individual Treatment Effect (ITE)
τ_i = E[Y_i(1) | X_i] - E[Y_i(0) | X_i]

Standard propensity models target users with high absolute conversion probability, inadvertently spending promotional budget on users who would transact anyway. GenCash models differential uplift (τ): targeting only users whose transaction depends strictly on receiving the incentive.

Gradient Boosted Trees RFM Segmentation Category Affinity Matrix

Sure Things Transact organically regardless Suppress (Save Budget)
Lost Causes Completely unresponsive Suppress (Zero Return)

Algorithmic Guardrails

Next-Best-Offer Ranking & Offer Fatigue Shield

Pairing multi-factor behavioral affinity with exponential decay suppression to protect customer engagement.

Multi-Objective Optimization
Composite NBO Ranking Formula
R(u, k) = τ̂_{u,k} × A_{u,c_k} × exp(-λ × F_u)
  • Uplift Propensity (τ̂): Estimated incremental transaction yield for user u on offer k.
  • Category Affinity (A): Historical expenditure concentration in matching merchant sector over 30 days.
  • Offer Fatigue Shield (exp(-λF)): Tracks repeated exposures without conversion to dynamically suppress spam.
  • Cooling-off Suppression: Automatically enters a 7-day silent period if fatigue exceeds threshold.
Explainable AI (XAI)
Deterministic Transparency
Black-box recommendations destroy customer trust. GenCash synthesizes human-readable rationales displayed directly on mobile offer cards:
"Recommended because 42% of your monthly expenditure was in retail groceries, and this Shwapno offer provides ৳ 50 cashback on orders over ৳ 500."
Dual synthesis in English and Bengali with zero generative hallucination.
Slide 8 Script (NBO & Fatigue): Point out our composite formula: Uplift Propensity times Category Affinity times the Exponential Fatigue Decay. GOVERNANCE & SAFETY Security & Ethical Guardrails
11 / 14
Reliability & Ethics

Financial Concurrency & Responsible AI

Strict mathematical isolation between deterministic financial accounting and probabilistic ML models.

Financial Integrity
Atomic Concurrency Locks
  • Row-level wallet locks prevent race conditions during concurrent P2P transfers.
  • Two-phase commit ensures ledger debit and credit succeed atomically or fail completely.
  • Zero possibility of phantom wallet balances or duplicate withdrawals.
Ethical AI Boundaries
Advisory Isolation
  • No Autonomous Credit Decisions: AI never issues credit rejections or loan approvals, preventing demographic bias.
  • Read-Only Boundary: ML models consume read-only telemetry; they can never directly manipulate ledger state.
  • Deterministic computation guarantees zero generative hallucination in balance figures.
Data Privacy
Synthetic Personas & Minimization
  • All training and demo datasets utilize calibrated synthetic personas reflecting Bangladeshi demographics.
  • Client-side PFM aggregations process only numerical amounts and timestamps.
  • No transmission of external contact books or private user SMS notes.
Slide 11 Script (Security & Responsible AI): Judges at fintech competitions scrutinize safety. Emphasize: 'We maintain strict mathematical isolation. AI is strictly an advisory copilot; it NEVER touches the double-entry accounting ledger directly, and it never issues autonomous loan approvals. Concurrency is protected by row-level database locks, preventing double-spend exploits.'
